# The Requester's guide

**My deals** shows your engagements and nobody else's. That is not a filter
applied in your browser — another buyer's deals never arrive here at all.

## Opening a deal

Give it a reference and the counterparty. There is no field for whose deal it
is, because it is yours: opening one "on behalf of" somebody else would put the
wrong name on every decision that follows from it.

## The pipeline rail belongs to one deal

Open a deal and the rail across its header — Intake, Manifest, Forge, Validate,
Dossier — is **that deal's** stages. In the old prototype the rail was one global
thing, so two deals at two stages shared it and the screen could only be telling
the truth about one of them.

## When a finding blocks you

**Asking is the only way past it.** There is no acknowledge button, no override
button, no proceed-anyway. The old one-click button is retired, and nothing here
replaces it.

What happens when you ask:

1. **You ask**, naming each finding, saying why in your own words, and citing any
   commercial pressure separately. It is recorded permanently and it is what the
   reviewer reads.
2. **The people who should know are told** — the deal owner and the watchers for
   those categories. If nobody would be told, your request is recorded but *not
   socialised*, and it cannot be decided until somebody fixes the watcher list.
   The screen says so rather than pretending it went out.
3. **A review window runs.** Its purpose is that nobody discovers the override at
   signature. Nothing can be decided while it is open.
4. **Legal decides each finding on its own.** Approving one does not approve the
   others.

**Asking is not being allowed.** Until a finding is approved it still blocks, and
the screen says so at every step — while socialising, while the window runs, and
after a rejection.

### Why the reason has to be a real one

The system refuses a justification shorter than twenty characters. That is not a
quality bar — nothing here can judge whether a reason is any good, and that
judgement belongs to the reviewer who reads it. It exists to stop "n/a".

### A rejected finding still blocks

The reviewer's note says why. Changing the contract is the way past it; asking
again is not.

## My record

Everything you have done, as the system recorded it. Only your own acts — the
rest of the record is not hidden from this page, it is never sent to it.

## What is not built yet

The intake interview, the manifest, the forge and the validate findings. Until
they are, you name the run and the findings you are asking about rather than
picking them from a list. That is deliberate: a list of invented findings would
be a screen claiming something the system cannot do.
