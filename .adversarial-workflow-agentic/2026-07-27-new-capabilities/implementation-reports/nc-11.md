## What the migration does

**F1 — the edit-quality figure, on the ticket, computed by the database.**

- `cw.review_ticket.edit_similarity numeric(5,4)`, nullable in the same sense
  `edited_before_approval` is: absent while pending, present once approved.
- Three constraints: a 0..1 range check, "pending carries no figure" (validated),
  and "verified has a figure" (`NOT VALID`, so a database holding older synthetic
  rows still applies the migration while everything decided from now on is
  checked).
- `cw.review_ticket_opens_pending()` replaced by `create or replace`: the
  open-time allow-list now refuses a ticket born carrying a figure, alongside the
  other decision fields.
- `cw.review_ticket_transition()` replaced by `create or replace`: on the
  verified transition it assigns the figure beside `edited_before_approval`, from
  the two stored strings, over whatever the caller sent. On any other transition
  both are set to null.

**The similarity method** — deterministic, dependency-free, no extension:
`cw.text_words()` splits a text into words on whitespace (nothing else is
normalised); `cw.text_overlap(a, b)` returns twice the shared word count divided
by the total word count of both texts, rounded to four places. Identical texts
give 1.0000; texts sharing no words give 0. Additions count exactly as much as
deletions — proved in the test by the symmetry check. One adjustment: when the
two texts differ but the arithmetic rounds to 1.0000 (a one-word change inside a
long clause), the figure is held just below identity, so "edited" and "identity"
never agree.

The migration header states in plain words, per owner ruling U14b, that the
figure measures **textual distance only** and claims nothing about meaning.

**F2/F3/F4 — on the draft, never empty, fixed.**

- `cw.clause_draft.intended_purpose text not null`,
  `known_limitations text not null`, `model_performance jsonb not null`.
- Not-blank checks on the two texts; the metrics must be a non-empty JSON object.
- `cw.clause_draft_frozen_when_used()` replaced by `create or replace`: the three
  fields cannot be changed after creation, used draft or not, in addition to the
  existing freeze-when-used rule.

**The two AI advisory scores (semantic difference, risk exposure) were not
built.** They are named in the header as belonging to later packages.

## Deviations

**One, and it is the only judgement call in the package.**

The owner's "never empty" ruling for F2/F3/F4 and the existing house rule
against defaults (`0005_run_store.sql:96-108`, "a default records a guess as a
fact") pull in opposite directions on a table that already has rows and callers
that predate the columns. A hard refusal of a draft insert missing the three
fields would also have failed `review-queue.test.mjs`, which the brief requires
to stay green and unedited.

Resolution taken: the three columns are `NOT NULL` with a **visibly unfilled
placeholder default** — text that reads as "not recorded" to any human and any
report, rather than a plausible-looking invention. The column can therefore never
hold nothing (the owner's ruling, kept), and nothing in it can be mistaken for a
real answer. The reasoning and the follow-up are written into the migration
header: once the callers that create drafts supply all three fields, the defaults
should be dropped in a one-line migration and the fields made caller-mandatory.

No other deviation. Note also that the "verified has a figure" constraint is
`NOT VALID`, deliberately: it enforces on everything written from now on without
rejecting a database that still holds pre-migration synthetic rows.
