The rules every screen obeys
Five inks, no sixth
effective pending refused never ran superseded
The words stay open — “awaiting countersign” and “window open” are different sentences in one ink, because they are one state. Red means error. Never “no”, never “severe”, and never an executed contract, however good the wax would look.

Set any page in greyscale and all five are still themselves. That is the test the four-pixel gutter failed, and the reason this direction exists.
Rail or row — one rule, no exemptions
Counted from the running app, not from taste.

Six or more gets a rail. The design proposal said “five or fewer keeps tabs” and then exempted the requester, who has six — a contradiction that survived into the first draft of this set and was caught only by looking at all eight together. One threshold now, applied everywhere.
How to look at the set
Serve it rather than opening the files directly: preview_start {name:"prototypes"}, then the contact sheet shows all eight at once.
Add ?grey to the contact sheet's address to drain the colour from every panel simultaneously — the fastest way to check that no screen has started leaning on hue to say something.
The contact sheet is a review tool, not a screen. It is prefixed with an underscore so it never reads as part of the set.
What is deliberately absent from all eight. No notification bell, no avatars, no onboarding tour, no confetti, no quick-approve, and no chart drawn for the pleasure of drawing one. Nothing blinks, pulses or flies in: two animations exist in the whole product — the stamp, when something becomes recorded, and the strike, when something is superseded. A still interface is the truthful one for a system whose promises are mostly about what it refuses to do.
Pictures of an interface, not builds of one — none of these files is ever served by the application. Every name, figure, clause, date and row across the set is invented, and all wording is placeholder. Typefaces are served from this repository (S226); opening any of these pages contacts nobody.
