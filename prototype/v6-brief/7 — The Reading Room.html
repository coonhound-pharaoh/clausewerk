Why this room is not empty by default
Colleagues being able to see each other's work is not a leak — people have to be able to cover for one another, and a record nobody can read is a filing cabinet nobody can audit.
Confidentiality is a per-deal decision somebody makes and signs for, not the silent default. When a deal is closed off, it is closed off by a named person for a recorded reason, and this room says so rather than simply going blank.
