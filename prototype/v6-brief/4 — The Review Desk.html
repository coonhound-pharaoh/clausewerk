A proposal is drawn in the waiting state until a named person decides it. The moment you decide, it stamps — and the stamp is the record, not this screen.
Your judgement — every act takes a reason, every time
Why this wording stands
Your reason is recorded in your own words, against your name, and travels with the clause for as long as it exists.
What you changed and why
An edited proposal is minted as your wording — the model's draft stays in the record as what was proposed, not as what was agreed.
Why it cannot stand
Refusing is an ordinary answer and is not drawn as an error. The clause does not enter the library and the requester is told.
Not available at this desk
You proposed the wording at ¶ 11.5 on 02 Aug. The database refuses a reviewer's decision on their own proposal, so this desk does not draw the act — the screen mirrors what the connection can actually do. TK-130 asks a second reviewer instead.
What this desk will not do
There is no acknowledge stamp. A finding that blocks stays blocking until it is decided — asking is not the same as being allowed, and an override is socialised, waits out its window, and is decided one finding at a time.
There is no quick approve. Every act above carries a reason field, because a decision without a recorded reason is an unattributable act, and this product exists to make acts attributable.
Nothing here is pre-filled. A screen that wrote your reason for you would be measuring its own suggestion.
