What this page refuses to do
Stated, so it stays refused.
Nothing blinks, pulses or flies in. Two animations exist in the whole product — the stamp, when something becomes recorded, and the strike, when something is superseded. There is no notification bell, no avatar, no quick-approve, and no chart drawn for the pleasure of drawing one. The waiting list above is the notification system, and it is honest about being empty when it is.
Why the margin and not the text. A mark inside the sentence competes with the sentence. The margin is where a lawyer already looks for the annotation, it has room for language rather than code, and it can be omitted from the export without touching a word of the document — which is what the 2026-07-25 decision requires. The origin of every word is on the screen; none of it is on the paper the counterparty signs.
