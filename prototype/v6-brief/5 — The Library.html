What this desk cannot do
It cannot edit a published version. There is no edit act in the database, so there is no edit button here — the screen is not being polite, it is mirroring what the connection can do.
It cannot unpublish. A supersession is an append, and the old version stays readable for as long as a contract quotes it.
It cannot publish alone. Publication binds the approver to the sitting, and the author and the approver cannot be the same name.
