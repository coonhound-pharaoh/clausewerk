People and access, and the health of the record.

This is the screen where pending must never look effective. Everywhere else that rule is good hygiene; here it is the control itself. A grant drawn as live before it is countersigned would hand someone a role nobody agreed to give them.

Grants
Every grant carries two names — the one who proposed it and the one who countersigned. They are never the same person, and the database is what refuses, not this screen.

A restore that has never been rehearsed is drawn as never rehearsed. The temptation to show it green because nothing has gone wrong is exactly the lie this product exists to refuse.
What an administrator cannot do
Cannot read a contract. This desk grants access to the record; it does not hold access to the deals. The screens for them are not drawn here because the connection could not fetch them.
Cannot grant themselves a role, and cannot countersign their own proposal.
Cannot delete a person. An account that acted has a history, and a history with the actor removed is not a record — it is an anecdote.
