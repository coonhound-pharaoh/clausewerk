What a report is, and is not
A report is a view of the record, never a second record. It is regenerated from the chain every time it is asked for.
If the chain does not verify, the report is refused rather than drawn with a caveat. A figure nobody can stand behind is worse than no figure.
Counts, ages and mixes are drawn as numbers and plain bars. There is no donut chart of clause categories here, because it would tell an auditor nothing they could act on.
A picture of an interface, not a build of one — never served by the application. Every figure, name, date, reference and row above is invented. Typefaces are served from this repository; opening this page contacts nobody.
