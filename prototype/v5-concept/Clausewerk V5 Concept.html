Everything on this page is invented — the names, the counts, the dates and the contract sentences. It is a picture of an interface, not a build of one, and the application itself still loads no example rows anywhere.

Why proposals are hatched
The same amber, the same hatch, everywhere in the product.
A field the model proposed uses the pending look — amber, hatched — and a field a person confirmed uses the effective look. When you confirm, it stamps solid. That single animation survives the cull because confirmation is the moment an opinion becomes an act, and it should feel like one.
